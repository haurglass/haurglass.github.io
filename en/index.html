Media Engine Publisher

Media Engine Publisher is a personal tool used only by its owner, Fernando, to publish the short videos the owner produces to the owner's own YouTube, Facebook and Instagram accounts.

What it does

The tool runs on its owner's computer, with no server and no online service. Starting from a finished video that the owner has already reviewed and approved, it does the following:

  • Uploads the video, with its title, to the owner's YouTube channel it was made for. The owner can connect several channels, all of them the owner's own.
  • Publishes it as a reel, with no text, on the owner's Facebook Page for that channel and on the Instagram professional account linked to that Page.
  • Schedules each publication at fixed times the owner defines, so the owner can review it and cancel it before it goes out.
  • Checks the status of every video it published to confirm it was published exactly once, because an interrupted upload is never blindly repeated.

On TikTok, the tool only sends the video to the inbox of the owner's TikTok account for that channel. The owner finishes the post in the TikTok app: writes its caption, chooses its privacy and publishes it.

Who uses it

Only its owner. Media Engine Publisher has no user sign-up, offers no accounts to other people, is not sold or distributed and does not provide a service to anyone else: it only publishes to accounts that belong to its owner.

What it asks Google, Meta and TikTok for, and why

The owner authorizes the tool with OAuth 2.0, the protocol Google, Meta and TikTok use to let an application act on an account without knowing its password. These are the only permissions it requests:

Permissions and their use
PermissionWhat it is used for
YouTube: youtube.uploadUpload the owner's videos to the owner's channels.
YouTube: youtube.readonlyConfirm that each authorized account is one of the owner's channels, and read its recent uploads and the status of each video the tool uploaded.
Facebook: pages_show_listFind the owner's Page among the Pages the owner manages.
Facebook: business_managementLet Facebook list the owner's Pages that belong to the owner's business portfolio. The tool reads no other business data.
Facebook: pages_read_engagementRead the Page's basic data, such as its linked Instagram account, and the status of published reels.
Facebook: pages_manage_postsPublish reels to the Page.
Instagram: instagram_basicRead the account's basic data and its recent posts, to confirm each publication without duplicating it.
Instagram: instagram_content_publishPublish reels to the account.
TikTok: user.info.basic and user.info.profileConfirm that the authorized account is the owner's configured TikTok account.
TikTok: video.uploadSend the owner's videos to the inbox of the owner's TikTok account, where the owner publishes them by hand.

The tool does not read statistics, comments, messages or followers. What it stores, where and for how long is described in the Privacy Policy.

Services it uses

Media Engine Publisher uses YouTube API Services, Meta's APIs for Facebook and Instagram, and TikTok's Login Kit and Content Posting API. Using it is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
