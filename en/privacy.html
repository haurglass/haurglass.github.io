Summary

  • Media Engine Publisher is used only by its owner, to publish the owner's own videos to the owner's own accounts, and it does not provide a service to anyone else.
  • All data is stored only on the owner's computer, and access tokens are stored encrypted.
  • Data obtained from Google and Meta is not sold or shared with anyone. Each video is sent only to the platform it is published to. There are no ads and no cookies.
  • You can revoke access at any time. Tokens and data obtained from the platforms are deleted as soon as the owner disconnects the account, and that data within 7 days at most from when the tool detects that access was revoked. The log of what the owner published to the owner's own accounts is kept as a record of those publications.

What the tool is and who uses it

Media Engine Publisher is a program that runs on its owner's computer and that the owner uses to publish the short videos the owner produces to three accounts that belong to the owner: a YouTube channel, a Facebook Page and the Instagram professional account linked to that Page. It has no user sign-up, offers no accounts to other people and does not provide a service to anyone else. The owner publishes TikTok videos by hand, so the tool does not connect to TikTok and obtains no data from it.

Use of YouTube API Services

Media Engine Publisher uses YouTube API Services to upload videos to the owner's channel and to check the status of those videos. Using the tool is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service (https://www.youtube.com/t/terms) and the Google Privacy Policy (http://www.google.com/policies/privacy).

Media Engine Publisher's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What it stores and where

Everything is stored only on the owner's computer, in a local database, and nothing is sent to servers of the owner or to cloud services. Specifically:

  • OAuth tokens, encrypted with AES-GCM (Advanced Encryption Standard in Galois/Counter Mode, an authenticated cipher) under a 256-bit key that only the owner holds and that is not stored with the data. Tokens exist decrypted only in memory, for the operation that uses them.
  • IDs of the owner's accounts (channel, Page and Instagram account) and their display names.
  • Data obtained from the platforms: for each video the tool published or tried to publish, the reference of each upload (the YouTube upload session address, the Instagram container or the Facebook video), which the tool needs to resume the upload or to check whether it was published; the ID and link the platform assigned to it; and the statuses observed on the platform (privacy, upload and processing). They are stored apart from the log so that they can be deleted, and the log keeps of each only a cryptographic fingerprint computed with a random value that is deleted with them, so once they are deleted the fingerprint no longer tells which video it was.
  • A log of the owner's publications: which short was published, when, on which platform and to which account, with which title, the result of each attempt and the scheduling, attempt and publication dates. It is the audit record of what the owner published to the owner's own accounts.
  • Technical logs of the tool's operation (which operation ran, its result and how long it took), which are written to the terminal or to the system log, outside the database, and never contain tokens, passwords, upload session addresses or the content of the platforms' responses.
  • A record of each token refresh and of each refusal of a token by the platform, with its date and result, without the tokens.

Who the data is shared with

  • With the destination platform, and only with it. When publishing, the tool sends YouTube, Facebook or Instagram the video file, the title (to YouTube only; it sends Facebook and Instagram no text) and the publication settings (for example, the YouTube privacy setting or the declaration of AI-generated content), and each platform handles them under its own policy.
  • No data is sold, rented or given to anyone. The tool shows no ads and does not allow third parties to serve content or ads in it.

Cookies and data on your device

Neither Media Engine Publisher nor this site uses cookies or similar technologies, analytics, ads, forms or third-party resources. The tool stores the data described above only on its owner's computer, the owner being its only user, and it does not store or read information on anyone else's device.

This site is static and hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub logs and stores the IP address of each visitor for security purposes, as explained in its privacy statement. The owner collects no data about visitors to this site.

How long data is kept

  • Tokens: while the account is connected. They are deleted immediately when the owner disconnects the account with media-engine publish account forget, which for Google first revokes the authorization (except with --sin-revocar). A revoked credential keeps its encrypted token until the owner runs that command, as explained in How to revoke access.
  • Data obtained from the platforms (reference of each upload, ID, link and observed statuses of each video): while the owner uses the tool and the account stays connected. It is deleted as soon as the owner disconnects the account with media-engine publish account forget, and within 7 days at most from when the tool detects that access was revoked: its periodic step (media-engine publish tick) deletes it as soon as it finds the account without a credential or with its credential revoked. The data of an upload in progress is kept until it is known whether it was published, because it is the only way to check, and it is deleted as soon as that is settled; if the outcome remains unknown, it is settled when the owner resolves it.
  • Refreshing YouTube data: while the tool remains in use, its periodic step (media-engine publish tick) reads again, every 25 days, the status of each video it published to YouTube, before the 30 days set by YouTube's policies have passed. Nothing more is read about an upload YouTube marked as failed, and its data is deleted 25 days after the last reading; the same happens to a video whose history admits no more readings.
  • Log of the owner's publications (which short the owner published, when, with which text and to which account): kept while the owner uses the tool, as the audit record of the owner's own publications, and not part of the 7-day deletion.
  • Technical logs: the tool does not keep them itself. It writes them to the terminal, or to the system log of the owner's computer when it runs on its timer, and that log discards them according to its own size and age settings.

How to revoke access

You can remove Media Engine Publisher's access at any time, without asking anyone:

  • Google and YouTube: on your Google Account security page, https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions, choose Media Engine Publisher and remove its access.
  • Facebook: in Settings & Privacy > Settings > Apps and Websites (https://www.facebook.com/settings?tab=applications). If the tool is listed as a business integration, remove it under Business Integrations (https://www.facebook.com/settings?tab=business_tools).
  • Instagram: in the Apps and Websites section of Instagram's settings (https://www.instagram.com/accounts/manage_access/). Because the tool publishes to Instagram through the Facebook Page, removing it on Facebook also removes its access to Instagram.

Once access is revoked, the stored tokens stop working immediately. The tool notices the next time it tries to use them and the platform refuses them, when refreshing a token, when publishing or when reading a video's status: it publishes nothing with them, marks the credential as revoked and holds that account's publications until the owner connects it again. Until it uses the access again, it does not know that access was revoked. A refusal the platform attributes to an expired token, or to a Facebook account that still has to be confirmed, is not taken for a revocation.

A revoked credential keeps its encrypted token, which no longer works, until the owner deletes it with media-engine publish account forget, and media-engine publish account status shows it as revoked and reminds the owner to delete it. Data obtained from that platform is deleted within 7 days at most from when the tool detects the revocation, and immediately when the owner runs that command. The log of what the owner published is kept as a record of those publications.

How to request deletion of your data

Anyone can write to haurglass28@gmail.com, with the subject "Data deletion", to ask whether the tool stores any data about them or to request its deletion. To handle the request, the owner disconnects the account with media-engine publish account forget, which immediately deletes the tokens and the data obtained from the platforms (that of an upload in progress, as soon as it is settled whether it was published), all within 7 calendar days at most, and the deletion is confirmed by email. The log of what the owner published to the owner's own accounts (which short, when, with which text and to which account) is not deleted by such a request: it is kept as the audit record of the owner's own publications. The full steps are in How to delete your data.

Deleting the data stored by Media Engine Publisher does not in any way affect data stored by YouTube, Facebook or Instagram: videos already published remain on those platforms, and deleting them requires using YouTube, Facebook or Instagram directly.

Security

The key that encrypts the tokens is held only by the owner, outside any code repository. Tokens and secrets are never written to logs or error messages, and the public repository of this site contains no credentials.
